How do I play?

Hold the joystick to move; release to stop at the next tile. Collect all diamond seeds and activate both lanterns, then reach the leaf gate. Shield blooms protect you for seven seconds. Dash recharges in 4.5 seconds.

Is there a time limit?

No. Finishing earns one star; no damage and beating the displayed target time each add a star. Gentle mode gives five hearts and slower sentries.

How is progress saved?

Progress is stored on the device, including the expedition in progress. Backgrounding pauses the game. No account or cloud sync is available. Deleting local progress cannot be undone; uninstalling can remove it.

Sound or controls?

Enable sound and music in Settings, check device volume and silent mode, and tap a game button to start audio. You can swap control sides, change joystick size and dead zone, reduce motion or disable haptics. Haptics depend on device support.

Language?

English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese follow the device’s first preferred language. Other languages use English. Restart the app after changing the system language.
