A little light. A winding way.

An original offline maze adventure. Guide a tiny garden rover, collect light seeds, restore two lanterns, and find the leaf gate. Explore 180 gardens across six regions, daily offline gardens and 30 local achievements.

Designed for Android, iPhone and iPad in landscape. English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese follow your device language. No account, advertising, analytics or in-app purchases.
