Offline by design

Glimseed is a single-player game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data or gameplay data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, tracking, account system, social features or in-app purchases. Gameplay never requires an internet connection. We do not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or advertising identifiers.

Data on your device

Level progress, stars, local achievements, settings and the current expedition are stored locally on your device. They are used only to run the game and restore your progress. We cannot access them. They remain until you erase progress in Settings or remove the app and its data. Device or operating-system backup services may keep copies according to your own backup settings; we do not operate a cloud-save service. Android application backup is disabled in this version.

Support email

If you choose to email kevinfunfk@gmail.com, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily send. We use them only to answer and resolve your request, not for advertising. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal data. Support correspondence is kept only as necessary to resolve the issue and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the same address; legal retention duties may apply.

External pages and service providers

The game includes an offline privacy summary. Opening the online privacy page or emailing support is optional and opens your browser or email application. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive standard request information, including IP addresses, for security and operation under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The website has no analytics, advertising, contact form or tracking cookies added by us. Email providers process messages under their own privacy terms. Store operators and your device OS may independently process download, diagnostic or purchase records under their policies; we do not integrate those services for data collection in this app.

Children and privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. No personal information is needed to play. If a child has sent personal information by email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal.

Your choices and changes

Use Settings → Erase local progress to remove local game data. For support correspondence or privacy questions, email kevinfunfk@gmail.com. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date and make any necessary disclosures or requests for consent before introducing the change.

离线游戏

本应用不收集、传输、出售或分享个人信息与游戏数据。应用不包含广告、分析统计、崩溃上报 SDK、追踪、账号、社交功能或应用内购买。游玩不需要网络，也不申请通讯录、相册、摄像头、麦克风、位置或广告标识权限。

外部页面与第三方

儿童、数据管理与变更

我们不会有意收集儿童个人信息，游玩无需提供个人信息。如儿童通过邮件提供了个人信息，监护人可以联系删除。你可通过“设置 → 清除本地进度”删除游戏存档。若数据处理方式改变，我们将更新政策与生效日期，并在引入变化前履行必要告知或同意程序。隐私联系邮箱：kevinfunfk@gmail.com。
