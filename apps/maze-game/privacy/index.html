ABOUT THIS POLICY

This policy covers Bomb Maze, an offline single-player maze game.

DATA HANDLED BY THE GAME

The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts, advertisements, purchases, analytics SDKs, or online game services. The app does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions.

LOCAL STORAGE AND DELETION

Stage progress, stars, high scores, preferences, and an unfinished game are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They remain until you remove them. Settings → Reset all progress deletes game progress, scores, stars, and the unfinished run; it keeps your preferences. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes all local app data. The app disables Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup in its configuration. It does not provide cloud sync.

VIBRATION

The vibration permission is used only for optional gameplay feedback. You can disable it in settings. It does not collect data.

SUPPORT AND EXTERNAL SERVICES

If you choose to contact support by email, the recipient receives the email address and message you send. These are used to respond to your request, retained only as necessary to handle it or meet applicable obligations, and can be the subject of a deletion request to the contact below. The game does not automatically attach device identifiers, saves, or diagnostics. Any browser, email app, app store, or operating-system services you use separately are governed by their own privacy terms.

CHILDREN

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players. It has no chat, user uploads, behavioral advertising, or account system.

POLICY CHANGES

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that functionality is introduced.

CONTACT

政策范围

本政策适用于《星火迷宫 / Bomb Maze》，一款离线单人迷宫游戏。

游戏处理的数据

游戏不收集、传输、出售或分享个人信息，不包含账户、广告、内购、分析 SDK 或在线游戏服务，不申请联网、相机、麦克风、通讯录或位置权限。

本地存储与删除

关卡进度、星星、最高得分、设置及未完成对局存放在设备的应用私有存储中，保留至你将其删除。“设置 → 清除全部进度”会删除游戏进度、分数、星星和未完成对局，同时保留偏好设置。在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载应用会删除全部本地应用数据。应用已在配置中禁用 Android 云备份及设备迁移备份，不提供云同步。

震动权限

震动权限仅用于可选的游戏触觉反馈，可在设置中关闭，不收集数据。

客服与外部服务

若你主动通过邮箱联系客服，收件方会收到你发送的邮箱地址和邮件内容。这些信息用于处理你的请求，仅在处理请求或履行适用义务所需的期间保留；你可以通过下方联系方式提出删除请求。游戏不会自动附加设备标识、存档或诊断数据。你另外使用的浏览器、邮件应用、应用商店或操作系统服务遵循各自的隐私政策。

儿童

游戏不会主动收集儿童或其他玩家的个人信息，不提供聊天、用户上传、行为广告或账户系统。

政策更新

若未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会在相关功能推出前更新本政策。
