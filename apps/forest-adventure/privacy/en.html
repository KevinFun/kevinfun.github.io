FOREST ADVENTURE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains how Forest Adventure: Jump & Run handles information. Publisher: KevinFun.

Offline gameplay

The game has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or in-app purchases. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising-ID permissions, and does not send gameplay data to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The game stores unlocked chapters, best stars, achievements, total fruit and defeated enemies, and sound, music, haptic and control preferences in its private local storage. These records provide progress and settings; they are not linked to a user account. The current run and campfire position are not restored after the app process ends.

Android backup and device transfer

Depending on your Android device, account and system settings, Android may back up progress and preferences to your system backup service or transfer them to another device. This is handled by Android and your backup provider, not by a Forest Adventure: Jump & Run server. The developer does not receive or access these backups. Manage backups through your device settings and backup provider.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you reset progress, clear app storage or uninstall the game. Reset progress removes gameplay records but keeps your preferences. To remove system backups as well, use your device or backup-provider controls; an in-game reset does not itself delete existing system backups.

Support and external links

If you choose to contact support, your email app handles the message. The recipient receives the address and information you choose to send; the game attaches no save files or device logs. Support messages are used to address your request and retained only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting support. Opening the online policy uses your browser, which follows its own privacy practices.

Children and safety

The game has no chat, public profiles or user-content sharing. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and remove it where required.

Changes to this policy

Changes to data practices will be reflected in the policy and its update date. Review the policy after app updates.
