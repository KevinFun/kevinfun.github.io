Lumacairn

A little light. A long journey.

An offline platform adventure across 48 stages and six forgotten regions. Jump, dash and wake the beacons of a sleeping coast.

Privacy policy · Política de privacidad · 隐私政策

Support

Email kevinfunfk@gmail.com with your device model, operating system, game version and the stage or steps where an issue occurred. Please do not include passwords or sensitive information.

Controls: left stick to move; ↑ to jump, including a second jump in the air; ◉ to attack and activate nearby beacons; ↠ to dash. Light three beacons and enter the stone arch. Every eighth stage has a keeper to calm.

Settings include sound, music, gentle mode, larger controls, left-handed controls and reduced motion. Progress saves automatically on this device. There is no account or cross-device cloud save.

Ayuda

Escribe a kevinfunfk@gmail.com e indica el dispositivo, el sistema, la versión y cómo reproducir el problema.

Usa la palanca para moverte, ↑ para saltar dos veces, ◉ para atacar y encender faros, y ↠ para impulsarte. Enciende tres faros y llega al arco. Cada octavo nivel tiene un guardián. Hay modo tranquilo, controles grandes y para zurdos. El progreso se guarda en el dispositivo, sin sincronización en la nube.
