Lumacairn · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026 · App: Lumacairn / 微光石境 · Developer: KevinFun

English

Lumacairn is an offline, single-player adventure game developed by KevinFun. This policy covers the Android and iOS apps with identifier xyz.kevinfun.lumacairn.

Information in the game

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, crash-reporting service, tracking, social features, in-app purchases or developer-operated server. It does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

Progress, achievements, settings and the current stage are stored locally using device storage. The system language is read locally to select English, Spanish or Simplified Chinese; it is not transmitted. Device operating systems may include app data in their own managed backups according to your system settings. The developer cannot access those backups.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you select Settings → Erase local progress, clear app data, or remove the app. Resetting progress deletes the current local save and settings. Operating-system backups are managed separately by you and your platform provider.

Support and external links

If you voluntarily email kevinfunfk@gmail.com, KevinFun receives your email address and the information you choose to send. This is used only to respond to and resolve your request, and retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. Email is handled by Google's email service.

Opening this policy or the support page opens your browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages with no added analytics, cookies or advertising scripts. GitHub and network providers may process technical request data such as IP addresses under their own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement. App stores and device operating systems also operate under their own privacy policies.

Children and security

The game does not knowingly collect children's personal information. It contains mild, stylized fantasy combat without blood. We minimize data by keeping gameplay offline. Please do not include sensitive information in support emails.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact KevinFun at kevinfunfk@gmail.com.

Español

Lumacairn es un juego de aventuras sin conexión y para un jugador, desarrollado por KevinFun. Esta política cubre las apps Android e iOS con identificador xyz.kevinfun.lumacairn.

La app no recopila, transmite, vende ni comparte información personal. No tiene cuentas, publicidad, análisis, servicios de informes de fallos, rastreo, funciones sociales, compras integradas ni servidores del desarrollador. No solicita acceso a contactos, ubicación, fotos, micrófono o cámara.

Los datos locales permanecen hasta borrarlos en Ajustes → Borrar progreso local, borrar los datos de la app o desinstalarla. Las copias de seguridad del sistema se gestionan por separado.
