Controls

Drag the floating joystick to move. Hold Strike to chain attacks. Heavy breaks shield armor. Dash grants brief invulnerability. Jump avoids ground attacks. Successful hits charge Pulse. Coral rings warn of enemy attacks.

Save data

Progress stays on this device. There is no account or cloud synchronization. Settings → Erase local progress deletes your save after confirmation. Uninstalling can erase progress. There are no ads or in-app purchases.

Controles y progreso

Mueve la palanca, mantén Golpe para encadenar ataques y usa Fuerte contra escudos. Esquiva y Salto evitan ataques; los golpes cargan Pulso. El progreso es local. Sin cuentas, nube, publicidad ni compras integradas.

操作与进度

拖动摇杆移动，长按连击，重击破盾，闪避与跳跃躲开攻击。命中积攒共振能量。进度仅保存在本机，无账号、云同步、广告和内购。可在设置中清除进度。
