English

This policy covers the Android and iOS game Brasswake: Offline Brawler (also listed as Brasswake: Pelea sin conexión / 铜潮 Brasswake：离线格斗), published by KevinFun, package identifier xyz.kevinfun.brasswake.

Data in the game

The game runs offline. KevinFun does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data from the app. There are no accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics, tracking, social features, in-app purchases or remote game servers. The app does not request access to contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos or advertising identifiers.

Local progress and deletion

Campaign progress, achievements, gameplay statistics, upgrades and settings are stored locally on your device. They are used only to operate the game. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Operating-system backup or device management may handle app data under your device settings; we cannot access those backups. To erase progress, open Settings → Erase local progress and confirm. Removing the app also removes its local data, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Progress cannot be recovered by KevinFun.

Optional support and external links

If you email kevinfunfk@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to respond to your request and address the reported issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive information. Messages are kept only as needed to handle support and any applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email.

The privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it in your browser sends ordinary web-request information, such as IP address and browser details, to the hosting provider. This website has no analytics scripts, advertising or forms. GitHub processes hosting data under its privacy statement. Your email provider may also process correspondence under its own policy. This external processing is separate from offline gameplay.

Audience, security and changes

This is a general-audience action game with stylized combat against machines and is not specifically directed to young children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the app. We minimize data by keeping gameplay on-device; protect your device using its built-in security settings. If this policy changes, we will update the date and this page. Material changes to app data practices will be reflected in the app and store disclosures.

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact KevinFun at kevinfunfk@gmail.com.
