Veyraforge Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 22, 2026. Applies to Veyraforge: Arena Fight, package / bundle identifier xyz.kevinfun.veyraforge, Android and iOS, version 1.0.0.

Who operates the game

Veyraforge is provided by KevinFun. Contact: kevinfunfk@gmail.com.

What the game stores

The game stores your chosen fighter, settings, level medals, counters and achievements in its private local storage. These records contain no name, email, account, advertising ID, location, contacts, photos or health data. They are used only to save your game and preferences.

No collection, sharing or tracking by the game

The game has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social feature, user-generated content, in-app purchases or game server. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo-library or tracking permission. Android uses vibration permission for optional haptic feedback, and does not request Internet permission.

Storage, backup and deletion

Local progress remains until you reset or remove it. Use Settings → Reset Save → Delete to erase local progress and settings, or uninstall the game. There is no developer cloud copy or cross-device sync. Android app backup is disabled. Your operating system or device backup tools may include iOS app data under their own settings and policies; KevinFun cannot access those backups. To remove a device backup, use the relevant system controls.

Optional support email

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide are processed to answer your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. Email is handled through our email provider, Google Gmail, under its own policies and may be processed in other countries. We retain support messages only as needed to resolve requests and generally delete them within 12 months after resolution, unless a legal obligation requires otherwise. You may request earlier deletion or access by emailing us.

External website

The privacy and support links open your browser only when you choose to open them. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard connection information such as IP address and server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. We add no advertising, analytics, cookies or tracking scripts. Browser, email and hosting providers are separate from the offline game.

Children and age rating

The game features stylized robot sparring, without blood, chat or gambling. It is not designed specifically for children and is not submitted to a Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. A guardian may contact us about information voluntarily provided in a support email. Follow the age rating shown by your store.

Your choices and changes

You can disable sound, music and haptics, play offline, reset your save, and avoid external links. Applicable access, correction or deletion requests concerning support correspondence can be sent to the address above. If future features change our data practices, we will update this policy and any required store disclosures before releasing them.
