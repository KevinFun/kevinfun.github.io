Help & saved data

Steer with the joystick, hold FIRE and use PULSE to clear nearby bullets. Rescue all three survivors, defeat the guardian and reach the exit. Progress is saved locally, including an unfinished mission checkpoint. Use Settings → Erase local data to reset the game. Android system backups are managed separately in your device settings.

For help, email kevinfunfk@gmail.com. Please describe the issue and your Android version. Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.
