Privacy Policy

Dunewake: Jackal Shooter · Boot Studio

Who we are

Boot Studio provides Dunewake: Jackal Shooter (Android package com.ember.Jackal). For privacy questions, support or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, contact kevinfunfk@gmail.com. Effective date: 21 September 2026.

Data in the game

The game is offline and has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or developer-operated servers. It does not request Internet access. We do not collect or transmit gameplay data, personal identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings from the game, and we do not sell game data.

Local saves, permissions and backup

Sector progress, scores, achievements, settings and an unfinished mission checkpoint are stored in private app storage on your device. The vibration permission is used only for optional haptic feedback. Android may back up or transfer saved game data according to your system and account settings; backup services are operated by your platform provider, not by Boot Studio. We cannot access or manage your system backups. Google Play and Android may separately process distribution or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies.

Your choices and deletion

Local data is kept until you use Settings → Erase local data, clear the app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game. Erasing data in the game removes local progress, achievements, settings and the current mission and cannot be undone. Android backups are separate: manage or delete them through your device or backup provider settings; restoring a backup may restore an older save. There is no game account to delete. App storage is protected by Android’s application sandbox; device and backup security also depend on your own settings.

Support messages and external services

Contact Support opens your email application; nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to provide, and use them to answer your request. Email is processed by the email providers involved, including Gmail. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for the request and reasonable follow-up or applicable legal obligations. Request deletion at kevinfunfk@gmail.com. Opening the online policy launches your browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under its privacy statement. We add no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this policy website.

Children and policy changes

The game is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. If you believe a child has supplied personal information in a support message, contact us to request deletion. We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change and show the revised effective date here. Material changes will also be explained in the app or its release information.
