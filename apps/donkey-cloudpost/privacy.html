Offline game

Donkey Cloudpost (com.donkeycloudpost.game) is published by KevinFun. The game runs offline and does not collect or transmit personal data, device identifiers or gameplay analytics. It has no accounts, advertising, purchases or tracking SDKs, and requests no Internet or sensitive runtime permissions.

Local progress and deletion

Progress, achievements, settings and the current game checkpoint are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. They are retained until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Game backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. We do not hold a server-side copy and cannot recover deleted saves.

Optional support

If you choose to email kevinfunfk@gmail.com, KevinFun receives your email address and the information you include. This is used only to answer your request and resolve the issue, not for advertising. Do not include passwords or sensitive documents. Support messages are deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved unless a longer period is required by law. You may request earlier deletion by email. Email providers process messages under their own policies.

Website and external services

The in-game policy is available offline. Opening the online version launches your browser. The public page is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security under its own privacy policy. This page contains no developer-added analytics, advertising, cookies or remote fonts. Browser and Google Play services operate under their own policies.

Children, security and sharing

The same offline data practices apply to all players, including children. KevinFun does not sell or share game data. Local files are protected by Android’s app sandbox; security also depends on your device. Support messages are accessed only to handle support or meet legal obligations. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children; a parent or guardian may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or support-message deletion, contact KevinFun at kevinfunfk@gmail.com. Material changes will be reflected in this policy with a new effective date. Any future collection or sharing feature will be disclosed before it is introduced.
