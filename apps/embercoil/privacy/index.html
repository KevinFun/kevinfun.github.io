Embercoil: Run & Gun

Effective date: September 22, 2026. Developer and data contact: KevinFun. Android package: xyz.kevinfun.embercoil.

An offline game

Embercoil is a single-player action game. The app has no accounts, advertising, purchases, analytics, social features, cloud saves or tracking SDKs. You can play without an internet connection.

Data on your device

Mission progress, scores, achievements and your settings are stored only in the app’s private local storage. The app reads the device language to select English, Spanish or Simplified Chinese, falling back to English for other languages. These values are not sent to KevinFun or other parties. The app does not request the Android internet permission.

Collection and sharing

The game does not collect or share personal or sensitive user data. It does not access your contacts, location, photos, files outside its private storage, microphone, camera, advertising ID or other installed apps. It does not sell data.

Permissions

The app uses the normal Android vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. You can turn this off in Settings. This permission does not collect information.

Retention, deletion and backup

Local progress remains until you erase it in Settings → Erase Local Progress, clear app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the game. In-game erasure also resets preferences and deletes the app’s local recovery copy. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled. We have no server copy of your progress and cannot recover it after deletion.

Website and support email

Only when you choose a policy or contact link does the game open your external browser or email app. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages over HTTPS, has no analytics or cookies added by KevinFun, and is publicly accessible without sign-in. GitHub may process technical request information, such as an IP address, under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send. We use it only to respond and handle your request, do not sell it or use it for marketing, and delete correspondence within 90 days after the issue is resolved unless longer retention is legally necessary. You may request earlier deletion by email.

Audience

The game features stylized machine combat, without blood or human injuries. It is intended for a general teen and adult audience, not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. Contact us if you believe such information was sent to us so we can delete it.

Security and changes

Local files are protected by Android’s app storage isolation. No transmission of gameplay data takes place. Changes to this policy will be published here with a new effective date. If data practices change in a future release, the app and its Google Play disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support email, contact KevinFun at kevinfunfk@gmail.com. For website hosting practices, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
