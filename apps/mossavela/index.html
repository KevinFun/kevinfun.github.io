About the game

Explore 48 islands across six regions, befriend 24 nature spirits, restore ancient beacons and earn 24 local milestones. Landscape joystick controls, turn-based encounters and a gentle original soundtrack. Fully offline; no ads, purchases or account required.

English, español and 简体中文 follow the device language. Unsupported languages use English.

How to play / Cómo jugar / 玩法

Move with the left joystick. Use Attune near beacons and spirits. Echo the glowing rune to restore a beacon. During encounters, Listen and Guard build trust; Bond at three trust or low unrest. Light three beacons and befriend one spirit, then reach the arch. Every eighth island has a keeper. Camp restores companions for free.

Usa el joystick para moverte y Afinar cerca de faros o espíritus. Repite la runa iluminada. Escuchar y Proteger generan confianza; Vincular con tres de confianza o poca inquietud. Activa tres faros y haz una amistad antes de llegar al arco. Cada octava isla tiene un guardián.

摇杆移动，靠近信标与灵体后点击共鸣。选择亮起的符文可点亮信标。遭遇时通过倾听与守护增加信任，达到 3 点信任或低躁动后即可结伴。点亮 3 座信标、结伴 1 只灵体后前往拱门；每第 8 岛有守望者。营地免费恢复伙伴。

Progress and reset / Progreso / 存档

Progress saves automatically on this device. There is no game cloud-sync service. Settings → Erase local progress permanently resets the journey. Do not uninstall if you want to keep local progress.

El progreso se guarda automáticamente en el dispositivo. No hay sincronización en la nube. Ajustes → Borrar progreso local reinicia el viaje.

游戏自动在本设备保存，无自有云同步。设置中的“清除本地进度”会永久重置旅程。需要保留进度时请不要卸载。

Controls / Controles / 操作设置

Settings includes music, sound, haptics, reduced motion, left-handed layout and larger controls. Switching away from the app pauses the game.

Los ajustes incluyen música, efectos, vibración, movimiento reducido, controles para zurdos y botones grandes.

设置提供音乐、音效、振动、减少动态效果、左手布局和加大按钮。切换到后台会自动暂停。
