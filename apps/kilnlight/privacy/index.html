Privacy policy

Effective date

22 September 2026. This policy applies to Kilnlight: Beacon Run (package xyz.kevinfun.kilnlight), developed and provided by KevinFun. Contact: kevinfunfk@gmail.com.

An offline game

The game has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, social features, in-app purchases or third-party data-collection SDKs. It does not request Android Internet permission. KevinFun does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data through the game.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, checkpoint state, stars, achievements and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are excluded in the app configuration. The game does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifier or other files.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you choose “Erase local progress” in Settings, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall. The in-game reset clears progress and preferences; Android storage deletion or uninstall removes all local app data. There is no online account to delete and no cloud restore service.

Contacting support

If you voluntarily email kevinfunfk@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use this information only to respond to and resolve your request, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable legal requirements. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

This website and external services

This policy and support website are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening this website is optional and takes place in your browser. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process IP addresses and technical request logs to deliver and secure the site according to their own privacy statement. We do not add cookies, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. Your email provider and Google Play separately process information under their own terms when you use those services.

Children and privacy

The game is designed for a general audience, not specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the game. A parent or guardian can contact us about support correspondence involving a child.

Changes and contact

We will update this public page and the effective date if our practices change. Any future feature that changes data handling will require an updated policy and store disclosure before release. Questions: kevinfunfk@gmail.com.
