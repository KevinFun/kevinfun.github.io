About this policy / 关于本政策

This Privacy Policy applies to Super Brick: Jump & Run (Chinese title: 超级积木：跳跃冒险), package com.brickquest.superbrick, provided by Boot Studio. Effective date: September 21, 2026.

本隐私政策适用于 Boot Studio 提供的 Super Brick: Jump & Run（中文名称：超级积木：跳跃冒险）。生效日期：2026 年 9 月 21 日。

Offline gameplay / 离线游戏

The game works offline. It has no account registration, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, or developer-operated gameplay server. The Android app does not request Internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or advertising-ID permissions. Gameplay data is not sent to Boot Studio.

游戏可离线运行，不设账号注册、广告、应用内购买、统计或追踪 SDK，也没有开发者运营的游戏服务器。Android 应用不申请网络、摄像头、麦克风、通讯录、位置或广告标识符权限。游戏数据不会上传至 Boot Studio。

Data stored on your device / 设备内保存的数据

The game stores completed levels, collected blueprints, best times, achievements, cumulative gameplay counters, a resumable level/checkpoint, and sound, music, vibration and control-size settings in private app storage. These records are used only to provide gameplay, restore progress and remember your preferences. They are not linked to an account or identifier supplied to us.

游戏会在应用私有存储中保存已完成关卡、蓝图数量、最佳时间、成就、累计游戏统计、可继续的关卡与检查点，以及音效、音乐、振动和按钮大小等设置。这些记录只用于提供游戏功能、恢复进度与记住偏好，不与提供给我们的账号或标识符关联。

Android backup and transfer / Android 备份与迁移

Android or your device provider may back up and transfer the app’s saved preferences, depending on your device and backup settings. Such backups are managed by the platform provider under its own terms and privacy policy; Boot Studio does not receive or control them. Restoring a device backup may restore an older save.

根据设备及系统备份设置，Android 或设备服务提供商可能备份和迁移应用存档。此类备份由平台提供商依据其条款与隐私政策管理，Boot Studio 不接收也不控制这些备份。恢复系统备份可能恢复旧存档。

Permissions / 权限

The app requests vibration permission solely for optional gameplay feedback. You can turn vibration off in Settings. No personal information is required to use the game.

应用仅申请振动权限，用于可选的游戏触觉反馈。你可以在设置中关闭振动。使用游戏无需提供个人信息。

Sharing and sale / 共享与出售

Boot Studio does not sell or share in-app gameplay data with advertisers or data brokers. Voluntary support correspondence may be processed by our email service provider to deliver and store messages. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect legal rights.

Boot Studio 不向广告商或数据经纪商出售、共享应用内游戏数据。主动发送的支持邮件可能由邮件服务提供商处理，以完成投递与存储。依法必须或为保护合法权利而必要时，我们可能披露相关信息。

Retention, deletion and your choices / 保存、删除与选择

Local saves remain until you delete them. Use Settings → Reset saved data to clear local progress and settings, or use Android’s app-storage controls. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, while platform-managed backups may remain under your device’s backup settings. The app’s reset action cannot delete platform backups. Manage or delete those backups with your device or platform provider.

We keep voluntary support correspondence only as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us at the email below to request access, correction or deletion of support messages, subject to applicable legal requirements. There is no game account to delete.

本地存档保留至你主动删除。可使用“设置 → 清除存档”，或 Android 应用存储设置清除本地进度与偏好。卸载通常会删除设备上的应用数据，但系统备份可能仍按设备备份设置保留；应用内清除操作不能删除平台备份，请通过设备或平台提供商管理。主动发送的支持邮件只保留至处理请求及履行适用法律义务所需期限。你可以通过下方邮箱请求访问、更正或删除支持邮件，适用法律另有要求的除外。本游戏没有需要注销的账号。

Children / 儿童隐私

The game does not ask children or other players to submit personal information and does not use ads or tracking. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion.

游戏不会要求儿童或其他玩家提交个人信息，也不使用广告或追踪技术。若家长或监护人认为儿童在支持邮件中发送了个人信息，可联系我们申请删除。

本地存档使用 Android 应用私有存储，政策网站采用 HTTPS。任何存储或传输方式都不能保证绝对安全。若功能或数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策、生效日期及应用内政策。
