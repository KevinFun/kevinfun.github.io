Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Puff Sprout: 2D Adventure is an offline, single-player Android game. No account, advertising, analytics SDK, or in-app purchase is included. The game does not send your gameplay data to Boot Studio.

1. App and developer

This policy applies to Puff Sprout: 2D Adventure (Chinese name: 噗噗芽：横版冒险), package com.puffsprout.adventure, published by Boot Studio. Privacy and support contact: kevinfunfk@gmail.com.

2. Data in the game

The game does not request an account or access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising ID or other persistent device identifiers. It has no internet permission and includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Game progress, achievements, collected items, best times, the current stage, and sound/control settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. These records are used only to operate the game and restore your progress. Boot Studio does not receive, sell, or share these records.

3. Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up the app’s saved progress and settings to your system backup provider or transfer them to another device, depending on your Android version, device manufacturer and backup settings. These operations are managed by Android and your backup provider, not a Boot Studio server. Boot Studio cannot access these backups. Manage or delete backups through your device and backup-provider settings.

4. Optional support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and resolve problems. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the address above.

5. This policy website and external services

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening the online page sends a normal web request to the hosting provider, which may process information such as your IP address and request logs for security and operation. This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms or embedded third-party media. GitHub’s own privacy statement applies to its hosting operations: GitHub General Privacy Statement. Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own policies and your settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until you clear the game’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall it, subject to Android backup and restoration behavior. Clear storage through Settings → Apps → Puff Sprout → Storage → Clear storage (labels vary by device). Manage any system backups separately. There is no in-game account to delete and no Boot Studio gameplay database.

7. Intended audience

The game is intended for players aged 13 and above. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support email, contact us so we can address it and delete it as appropriate.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change. Contact Boot Studio at kevinfunfk@gmail.com for privacy questions, deletion requests concerning support email, or game support.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 21 日

1. 应用与开发者

本政策适用于 Boot Studio 发布的 Puff Sprout: 2D Adventure（噗噗芽：横版冒险），包名为 com.puffsprout.adventure。隐私与支持邮箱：kevinfunfk@gmail.com。

2. 游戏中的数据

本游戏为离线单机游戏，无账号、广告或应用内购买，不申请网络权限，不包含广告、分析或第三方崩溃上报 SDK，不访问位置、联系人、相机、麦克风、照片、广告标识符或其他持久设备标识符。

关卡进度、成就、收集记录、最佳用时、当前关卡及音效和操作设置，仅保存在应用的设备私有存储内，用于游戏运行和恢复进度。Boot Studio 不接收、出售或共享这些游戏记录。

3. 系统备份和设备迁移

根据 Android 版本、设备厂商和备份设置，Android 可能将存档和设置备份至系统备份服务，或迁移到另一台设备。这些操作由 Android 和备份服务提供方管理，而非 Boot Studio 服务器；我们无法访问这些备份。请在设备及备份服务的设置中管理或删除备份。

4. 自愿发送的支持邮件

如果您主动联系支持邮箱，我们会收到您的邮箱地址及您选择提供的信息，仅用于答复请求和解决问题。请勿发送密码、支付信息或其他不必要的敏感资料。邮件通过我们的邮件服务提供方处理，保留时间以处理请求及适用义务所需为限。您可通过上述邮箱申请删除相关支持邮件。

5. 政策网站与外部服务

在线政策由 GitHub Pages 托管。打开网页会向托管方发送普通网络请求；托管方可能为安全及运行目的处理 IP 地址和请求日志。本政策页面不含广告、统计脚本、跟踪像素、表单或第三方嵌入媒体。托管服务适用 GitHub 隐私声明。Google Play 和 Android 也可能依据其自身政策及您的设置单独处理信息。

6. 保留与删除

本地记录保留至您清除游戏存储或卸载应用，系统备份及还原行为可能影响后续恢复。您可以进入 Android「设置 → 应用 → Puff Sprout → 存储 → 清除存储」（不同设备名称可能不同）删除本地数据；系统备份需要单独管理。本游戏没有需要注销的账号，也没有 Boot Studio 游戏数据库。

本游戏面向 13 岁及以上玩家，不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果您认为儿童通过支持邮件向我们发送了个人信息，请联系上述邮箱，我们会核实处理并按需删除。

8. 更新与联系

如数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页和生效日期。隐私问题、支持邮件删除请求及游戏支持均可联系 Boot Studio：kevinfunfk@gmail.com。
